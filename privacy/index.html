Orbit Glide Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Orbit Glide ("the game") is made by William Blaine Smith ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and your choices. We keep it simple: we don't run servers or accounts, and we never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, Stardust, unlocked comets, levels, missions and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers, your approximate location (from your IP address), and how you interact with ads.

  • Orbit Glide does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps (App Tracking Transparency), so your device's advertising identifier is not used for personalized ads.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and some US states), you are asked for your consent, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off ads between runs.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm and restore purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Game Center (Apple)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics

The game does not currently send gameplay analytics to us or to any analytics service. If we add anonymous analytics in the future (to learn, for example, where players get stuck), we will update this policy first.

Children

Orbit Glide is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your game data: Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.
  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy choices (where available).
  • Turn off Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
