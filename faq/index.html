Orbit Glide FAQ

Quick answers about Orbit Glide, the one-tap space game for iPhone.

Get Orbit Glide free on the App Store

Is Orbit Glide free?

Yes. It's free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases (Stardust packs and a one-time No Ads purchase), and ads show between some runs.

Does Orbit Glide work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays without Wi-Fi or mobile data, so it works on planes, subways and anywhere without signal. Ads, purchases and Game Center leaderboards need a connection. More about playing offline.

How do you play?

Your comet orbits a planet. Tap once to let go, and it flies off in a straight line toward the next planet. Land on it to keep going. Each planet starts collapsing after you arrive, so you can't wait forever.

How do you get a PERFECT?

Land in the exact center of the next planet. Watch the dotted aim line while you orbit and tap when it points straight through the next planet's center. More tips.

What is a GODLIKE streak?

PERFECTs in a row build a streak. The callouts are NICE at 3, AMAZING at 5, STELLAR at 8, COSMIC at 12 and GODLIKE at 16.

How many comets are there?

100: 25 Common, 25 Rare, 20 Epic, 15 Legendary and 15 Special. Each has its own look and trail. See them all.

How does the Comet Wheel work?

Spend Stardust you collect while playing to spin the wheel and unlock a comet you don't own yet. The odds are shown in the game: Common 60%, Rare 25%, Epic 10%, Legendary 5%. You never get a duplicate, and there's one free spin a day for watching an ad.

Are there levels?

Yes: 100 levels plus 100 Upside Down levels, along with endless mode and a Daily Challenge. Levels and hazards.

How do I remove ads?

Buy No Ads in the Shop. It's a one-time purchase that removes the ads between runs.

Is Orbit Glide on Android?

Not yet. It's on iPhone, and you can try a free demo in your browser.
