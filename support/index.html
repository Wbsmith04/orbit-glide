Common questions

  • I bought something but don't have it. Open Settings or the Shop and tap Restore purchases.
  • How do I remove ads? Buy Remove Ads or the Starter Pack in the Shop. Optional reward ads stay available if you want them.
  • How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset progress. This can't be undone.
  • Ad privacy choices: Settings → Privacy choices.
