Orbit Glide tips: how to get PERFECT streaks

Orbit Glide is a one-tap arcade game for iPhone. Your comet orbits a planet, and one tap sends it flying toward the next. Here's how to score higher.

Get Orbit Glide free on the App Store

1. Aim for dead center

Landing in the middle of the next planet's ring scores a PERFECT. Watch the dotted line in front of your comet while it orbits: tap when it points straight through the center of the next planet.

2. Chain PERFECTs into streaks

Every PERFECT in a row grows your streak, and the game calls out each milestone:

StreakCallout
3NICE!
5AMAZING!
8STELLAR!
12COSMIC!
16GODLIKE!

3. Don't wait too long

Each planet starts collapsing after you arrive. The ring around it shows how much time you have left. Release before it runs out, even if the angle isn't perfect.

4. Grab QUICK bonuses

Fling early in your orbit, before you've gone all the way around, and you'll see QUICK +1. The bonus only counts if you land, so don't rush a bad angle just for it.

5. Watch for hazards

  • Red rocks (debris): touching one ends your run, so time your fling to pass between them.
  • Gravity wells: they bend your path when you fly close, so aim a little to the other side.
  • Wormholes: they warp you ahead along the path.

6. Play the Daily Challenge

Every day there's one challenge with the same planets for everyone, so you can compare scores fairly with friends. Your best runs also go to the Game Center leaderboards.

7. Collect comets

There are 100 comets to collect, from glossy pebbles to black holes and blazing suns, each with its own trail. Collect Stardust while you play and spend it on the Comet Wheel.
