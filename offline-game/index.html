A free offline game for iPhone: Orbit Glide

Orbit Glide is a one-tap space arcade game that plays fully offline. No Wi-Fi, no mobile data, no problem. Download it once and play on a plane, on the subway or on a road trip.

Get Orbit Glide free on the App Store

What you do

Your comet orbits a planet. Tap once to let go and it flies toward the next one. Land dead center for a PERFECT and chain them into streaks, all the way up to GODLIKE. Every planet starts collapsing after you land, so you have to keep moving.

Why it's good when you're offline

  • Everything runs on your phone. Endless mode, all 200 levels (100 plus 100 Upside Down), the Daily Challenge and the comet collection all work without a connection.
  • Short runs. A run takes a minute or two, so it fits into a wait at the gate or a few subway stops.
  • One hand, one tap. Easy to play standing up or squeezed into an airplane seat.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, so you pick up right where you left off.

A connection is only needed for ads, purchases and posting scores to the Game Center leaderboards.

Things to unlock

Collect Stardust on the way between planets and spend it on the Comet Wheel. There are 100 comets to collect, from glossy pebbles to black holes and blazing suns, each with its own trail.
