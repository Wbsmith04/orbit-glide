Orbit Glide has 100 comets. 85 come from the Comet Wheel, and 15 are Special: you can't spin for them, you earn them by completing a challenge.

Tips

  • Lucky Clover and Ghost Runner come from Perfects: aim for the dead center of each planet. See how to get PERFECT streaks.
  • Snowglobe needs the Daily Challenge seven days in a row, so play it every day.
  • Error 404 is the hardest: it needs the last level of Upside Down mode.
  • The Final Pebble is the last comet you'll get. It needs every comet from the Comet Wheel.
