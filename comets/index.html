All 100 Orbit Glide comets and how to unlock them

Orbit Glide has 100 comets to collect. 81 come from the Comet Wheel, which you spin with Stardust collected while playing; every spin gives you a comet you don't own yet, and the odds are shown on the wheel screen. 4 come with Shop packs, and the other 15 are special comets you earn by completing challenges.

Tips for collecting

  • Pick up Stardust during runs; it's the only thing you need for the Comet Wheel.
  • The wheel never gives a duplicate, so every spin is a new comet.
  • Special comets reward skill and habits: PERFECT streaks, close calls, levels beaten and daily streaks.
