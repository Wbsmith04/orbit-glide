Description

Your comet orbits a planet. Tap to let go. Orbit Glide is a one-touch arcade game about perfect timing: release at the right moment to slingshot across space and land on the next planet. Hit dead center for a PERFECT and chain PERFECTs into streaks, but keep moving, because every planet collapses moments after you arrive.

Features

  • One-tap controls, easy to learn and hard to master
  • PERFECT streaks that multiply your score
  • 200 levels with ghost planets, gravity wells and wormholes
  • A daily challenge with the same planets for everyone
  • 100 collectible cosmetic comets, each with its own trail
  • Game Center leaderboards; plays offline
