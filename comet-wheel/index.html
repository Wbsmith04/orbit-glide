The Comet Wheel: odds, prices and free spins

The Comet Wheel is how you unlock most of Orbit Glide's 100 comets. You spin it with Stardust, the gold shards you collect between planets.

Get Orbit Glide free on the App Store

Odds

These are the odds shown on the wheel in the game:

RarityChance per spin
Common60%
Rare25%
Epic10%
Legendary5%

No duplicates: every spin gives you a comet you don't own yet.

What a spin costs

  • Your first spin costs 60 Stardust.
  • After that, spins start at 150 and go up by 50 each time, up to a maximum of 500.
  • You get one free spin a day by watching a short ad.

How to earn Stardust faster

  • Fly through the shards on the way between planets; bonus clusters are worth more.
  • Complete missions and the Daily Challenge for extra rewards.
  • Longer runs mean more planets and more Stardust, so work on your PERFECT streaks.

What the wheel doesn't give

The 15 Special comets aren't on the wheel. You earn each one by completing a challenge.
