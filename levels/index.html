Orbit Glide levels, hazards and Upside Down mode

Orbit Glide has 100 levels, plus 100 more in Upside Down mode. Each level is a race to a finish planet, and a new hazard is introduced every few levels.

How to unlock Upside Down mode

Beat all 100 levels to unlock Upside Down. You play the same kind of levels flipped: your comet flies down the screen, and they're harder. Beating the last level of each set also unlocks a rare comet.

The Daily Challenge

Every day there's a new challenge with the same planets for every player, so scores are a fair comparison. Your best daily score goes to a Game Center leaderboard that resets each day.
